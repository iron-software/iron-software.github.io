    public sealed class CompoundLineValues : Enum

    CompoundLineValues sets how many parallel strokes make up a line or border. Simple is the single solid stroke and the usual choice, Double and Triple draw two or three equal parallel lines, and ThickThin and ThinThick pair a heavy stroke with a light one in the given order. Choose the value that matches the compound outline you want on a shape or border.

    var compound = CompoundLineValues.Simple;

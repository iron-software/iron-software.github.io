    Position content across the width of a shape or cell with HorizontalAlignment. Left aligns to the left edge, Center centers content within the available width, and Right aligns to the right edge. Reach for it when placing text, images, or table content where left-to-right position matters on a slide. The add text how-to covers placing and styling text, and the add shape example positions shapes on a slide.

    var align = HorizontalAlignment.Center;

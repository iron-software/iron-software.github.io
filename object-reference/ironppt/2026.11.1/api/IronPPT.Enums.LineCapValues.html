    public sealed class LineCapValues : Enum

    When a line or outline ends, LineCapValues decides the shape of that endpoint. Flat cuts the line off square at the exact end point, Round adds a semicircular cap that extends past it, and Square adds a squared cap that also extends past the end. Choose the cap that suits a stroke or text outline. The add shape example draws shapes on a slide, and TextOutlineEffect applies a line cap to outlined text.

    var cap = LineCapValues.Round;

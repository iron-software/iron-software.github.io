## Injected overview (Markdown)

Marking the role a section plays in a presentation runs through `DocumentSectionType`. `Default` is the ordinary body section and the usual value, `TitlePage` flags the opening title section, `Index` marks a contents or index section, and `Appendix` marks supplementary material at the end. Assign the value that describes a section's purpose in the deck.

```csharp
var sectionType = DocumentSectionType.Default;
```

## Injected overview (Markdown)

Position content across the width of a shape or cell with `HorizontalAlignment`. `Left` aligns to the left edge, `Center` centers content within the available width, and `Right` aligns to the right edge. Reach for it when placing text, images, or table content where left-to-right position matters on a slide. The [add text how-to](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/how-to/add-text/) covers placing and styling text, and the [add shape example](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/examples/add-shape/) positions shapes on a slide.

```csharp
var align = HorizontalAlignment.Center;
```

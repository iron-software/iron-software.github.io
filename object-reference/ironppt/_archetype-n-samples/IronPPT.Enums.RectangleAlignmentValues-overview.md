## Injected overview (Markdown)

Anchor content to a corner, edge, or the middle of its rectangular area with `RectangleAlignmentValues` in IronPPT. `Center` pins to the middle, the four edge values `Left`, `Right`, `Top`, and `Bottom` anchor to a side, and the corner values `TopLeft`, `TopRight`, `BottomLeft`, and `BottomRight` pin to a corner. `None` applies no specific anchor. Pick the value when positioning a fill, image, or shape within its bounding box. The [add shape example](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/examples/add-shape/) shows shapes positioned on a slide.

```csharp
var alignment = RectangleAlignmentValues.Center;
```

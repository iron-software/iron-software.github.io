## Injected overview (Markdown)

Apply a built-in PowerPoint table look with `TableStyle` in IronPPT instead of formatting every cell by hand. `TableNormal` is the plain unstyled baseline and `TableGrid` adds simple gridlines, while `LightShading`, `LightList`, `MediumShading1`, and `DarkList` step up through banded and emphasized presets. Choose the preset that matches the deck's emphasis when you add a table, and IronPPT writes the corresponding style into the saved presentation.

```csharp
var tableStyle = TableStyle.TableGrid;
```

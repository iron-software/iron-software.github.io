## Injected overview (Markdown)

Anchoring content to the top, center, or bottom of its vertical space uses `VerticalAlign`. `Top` pins content to the upper edge, `Middle` centers it within the available height, and `Bottom` drops it to the lower edge. Reach for it when positioning a slide element relative to the band it occupies. The [add shape example](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/examples/add-shape/) places elements on a slide.

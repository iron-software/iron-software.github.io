## Injected overview (Markdown)

Label what kind of content a section holds with `DocumentSectionType`. `Default` is the ordinary body section and the value most sections use, `TitlePage` marks an opening title section, `Index` flags an index region, and `Appendix` denotes supplementary material at the end. Choose the value that matches the role a section plays in the document. The [document element tutorial](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/tutorials/document-element/) walks through how sections and other elements fit together.

```csharp
var sectionType = DocumentSectionType.TitlePage;
```

## Injected overview (Markdown)

Pick the dash pattern an outline stroke draws by setting `PresetLineDashValues` on `TextOutlineEffect.presetLineDash`. `Solid` is the default continuous line, `Dash` and `Dot` give simple repeated dashes or dots, and `DashDot` alternates the two. Longer variants such as `LongDash`, `LongDashDot`, and `LongDashDotDot` stretch the pattern, while the `Sys` and `System` forms map to the platform's built-in dash styles. The [text outline effect how-to](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/how-to/text-effect-text-outline-effect/) walks through applying an outline.

## Injected overview (Markdown)

When two segments of a shape or text outline meet, `StrokeJoinStyleValues` decides how the corner is rendered. `Round` produces a softened, curved join, `Bevel` flattens the corner into a short straight edge, and `Miter` extends the edges to a sharp point. The [text outline effect guide](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/how-to/text-effect-text-outline-effect/) shows outline styling applied to document text.

```csharp
outline.JoinStyle = StrokeJoinStyleValues.Round;
```

## Injected overview (Markdown)

Anchoring a shape to the top, center, or bottom of its frame runs through `VerticalAlignment`, the value assigned to `Shape.VerticalAlignment`. `Top` aligns the shape to the upper edge, `Middle` centers it vertically, and `Bottom` aligns it to the lower edge. This is the shape-side counterpart to the broader `VerticalAlign` enum. The [add image to Word document](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/how-to/add-image/) walkthrough places visual elements on a page.

```csharp
shape.VerticalAlignment = VerticalAlignment.Top;
```

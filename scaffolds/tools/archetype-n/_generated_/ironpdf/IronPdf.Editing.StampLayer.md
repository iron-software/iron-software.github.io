## Injected overview (Markdown)

The `StampLayer` enumeration in IronPDF controls whether a stamp is placed above or below the existing page content of a PDF.

Part of the `IronPdf.Editing` namespace, it declares two values: `OnTopOfExistingPDFContent` and `BehindExistingPDFContent`.

Set the layer on any `Stamper` to overlay logos, signatures, and headers, or to push watermarks beneath the original page. See [Stamp Text & Images](https://ironpdf.com/how-to/stamp-text-image/) for typical C# usage.

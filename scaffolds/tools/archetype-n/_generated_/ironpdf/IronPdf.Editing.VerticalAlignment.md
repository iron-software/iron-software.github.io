## Injected overview (Markdown)

The `VerticalAlignment` enumeration in IronPDF declares vertical placement of stamps, watermarks, and overlay content relative to the page.

Part of the `IronPdf.Editing` namespace, it includes `Top`, `Middle`, and `Bottom` (3 total).

Pair with `HorizontalAlignment` to anchor a stamp on the canvas through the 3x3 placement grid. See [Custom Watermarks](https://ironpdf.com/how-to/custom-watermark/) for `ApplyStamp` and `ApplyWatermark` positioning patterns in C#.

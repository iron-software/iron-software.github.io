## Injected overview (Markdown)

The `PdfAVersions` enumeration in IronPDF declares the PDF/A archival profile applied by `ConvertToPdfA`.

Part of the `IronPdf` namespace, the canonical Basic-compliance levels lead the set: `PdfA1b`, `PdfA2b`, and `PdfA3b`, with PDF/A-3b being the default for ZUGFeRD hybrid invoices. Accessibility (`a`) and engineering (`PdfA4`, `PdfA4e`, `PdfA4f`) variants complete the 9 total members.

See [export PDF/A documents in C#](https://ironpdf.com/how-to/pdfa/) for usage.

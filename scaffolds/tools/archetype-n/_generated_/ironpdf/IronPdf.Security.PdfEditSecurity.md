## Injected overview (Markdown)

The `PdfEditSecurity` enumeration in IronPDF controls what edits an owner-password holder can perform on an encrypted PDF.

Part of the `IronPdf.Security` namespace, the 3 members are `NoEdit`, `EditPages`, and `EditAll`, covering the restriction tiers most workflows need. Assign a member to `PdfSecuritySettings.AllowUserEdits` before saving the document.

See [Set PDF Passwords and Permissions](https://ironpdf.com/how-to/pdf-permissions-passwords/) for end-to-end C# usage.

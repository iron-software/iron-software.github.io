## Injected overview (Markdown)

The `PdfPrintSecurity` enumeration in IronPDF controls what printing a viewer may perform on a secured document.

Part of the `IronPdf.Security` namespace, the 3 members are `NoPrint`, `PrintLowQuality`, and `FullPrintRights`, covering blocked output, draft-only output, and unrestricted printing. Assign a member to `PdfSecuritySettings.AllowUserPrinting` before encrypting and saving the file.

See [Set PDF Passwords and Permissions](https://ironpdf.com/how-to/pdf-permissions-passwords/) for full C# usage.

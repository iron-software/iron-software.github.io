## Injected overview (Markdown)

`BorderValues` names the line style drawn for a border edge. `None` (and `Nil`) leaves the edge blank, `Single` is the plain solid rule and the everyday choice, and `Double`, `Triple`, `Thick`, `Dotted`, and `Dashed` cover the common variations. The remaining members supply the full PowerPoint catalog of patterned and three-dimensional borders, such as `DotDash`, `DashSmallGap`, `Wave`, `DoubleWave`, `ThreeDEmboss`, and the layered `ThinThick` and `ThickThin` combinations. Choose the value that matches the border appearance you need.

```csharp
var border = BorderValues.Single;
```

The [add shape example](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/examples/add-shape/) creates an element you can give a border.

## Injected overview (Markdown)

When a line or outline ends, `LineCapValues` decides the shape of that endpoint. `Flat` cuts the line off square at the exact end point, `Round` adds a semicircular cap that extends past it, and `Square` adds a squared cap that also extends past the end. Choose the cap that suits a stroke or text outline. The [add shape example](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/examples/add-shape/) draws shapes on a slide, and `TextOutlineEffect` applies a line cap to outlined text.

```csharp
var cap = LineCapValues.Round;
```

## Injected overview (Markdown)

Controlling how surrounding text flows around a shape or image uses `WrapText`. `Square` wraps text around the bounding box and `Tight` and `Through` hug the actual outline more closely, while `TopAndBottom` keeps text clear above and below only. `BehindText` and `InFrontOfText` place the object on its own layer with no wrapping, and `InLineWithText` treats it as a character in the line. `None` applies no wrapping rule. The [manage image how-to](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/ppt/how-to/manage-image/) covers placing images on a slide.

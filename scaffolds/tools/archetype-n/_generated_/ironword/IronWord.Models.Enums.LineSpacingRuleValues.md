## Injected overview (Markdown)

Control how IronWord interprets the line spacing on a paragraph by setting `LineSpacingRuleValues` on `SpacingBetweenLines.LineRule`. `Auto` is the usual choice and lets the spacing scale with the font, `AtLeast` treats the value as a minimum that grows when content needs more room, and `Exact` pins the line height to a fixed measurement regardless of font size.

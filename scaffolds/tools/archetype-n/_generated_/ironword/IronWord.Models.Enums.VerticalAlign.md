## Injected overview (Markdown)

Position content against the top, center, or bottom of its container by choosing a `VerticalAlign` member. `Top` anchors content to the upper edge, `Middle` centers it between the edges, and `Bottom` pins it to the lower edge. Reach for this short three-value enum wherever a vertical placement choice is needed. The [add a table](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/how-to/add-table/) walkthrough shows cell content positioning in context.

```csharp
var align = VerticalAlign.Middle;
```
